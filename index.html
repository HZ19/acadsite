I work on the quantitative side of political science and social science: applied research methods, econometrics, causal inference, and increasingly data- and computation-heavy techniques. I teach these tools as directly as I use them.

I'm a quantitative social scientist with doctoral training in applied statistics and econometrics from the European University Institute. My research sits in political economy, political finance, and economic history, with a growing computational and data-intensive edge. I currently work as an Associate Research Scientist in the Division of Social Science at NYU Abu Dhabi. I've taught graduate causal inference at EUI, led undergraduate tutorials at Oxford, and I mentor NYUAD undergraduates in statistical computing and reproducible research.
